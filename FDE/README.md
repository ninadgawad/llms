## FDE-  Forward Deployed Engineer
An FDE is a customer-facing software engineer who embeds directly into a client's environment. 
Instead of working on a remote, internal staging server, FDEs build, integrate, and debug code directly inside the live, often messy operational systems of their enterprise clients.


### Software Engineer:
A traditional SWE’s week centers on **predictability and deep focus**. 
They look at a product roadmap, take a scoped feature request out of the backlog, write code, review pull requests, and optimize system uptime. 
Their measure of success is a merged pull request that safely reaches millions of abstract users. 


###  Forward Deployed Engineer:
An FDE’s week centers on variety and adaptation. On Monday, they might be in a boardroom explaining technical limitations to a client's VP.
By Wednesday, they are writing custom Python scripts to bridge their product with a client’s undocumented legacy database.
On Friday, they are fixing a live bug on the client's system before a major go-live deadline. 
Their measure of success is the client successfully running the platform to **solve their business problems**.
